# Florida Notice of Commencement Integration Roadmap (Post‑2023)
> A tight, Florida‑specific analogue to your Texas RCDS research brief. All citations reference **Fla. Stat. Ch. 713 (Part I)** and **Ch. 558** as currently in force.

---
## 1 Statutory Cross‑Walk
| Texas Provision | Florida Counterpart | Trigger | Required Content & Formatting | Penalties / Consequences |
|-----------------|---------------------|---------|-------------------------------|-------------------------|
| **§ 53.255 RCDS**<br>“Know Your Rights” notice | **§ 713.13(1)(d)‑(e)**<br>Notice of Commencement (NOC) + bold statutory warning | *Before first inspection / work*<br>(must be recorded & posted pre‑start) | Statutory form; owner signature; bold‑face warning re: improper payment; legal description; contractor, lender, surety | Payments after expired/unrecorded NOC deemed **improper** → owner can pay twice; liens obtain later priority; § 713.345 false‑info = 1st‑deg. misdemeanor |
| **§ 53.256 Sub list** | **§ 713.13(1)(d)7 & § 713.06(2)(c)** owner’s right to list of subs/suppliers | Owner may demand list **any time**; GC must respond within **10 days** | Names/addresses of all subs + suppliers on job | Failure → owner may withhold payment; GC commits 1st‑deg. misdemeanor (§ 713.345) |
| **§ 53.258 Progress aff.** | **§ 713.06(3)(d)** Interim payment affidavit | With each draw *if requested* or as contract requires | Sworn statement that subs/suppliers paid to date | False affidavit → lienor liability + perjury; owner may withhold payment |
| **§ 53.259 Final aff.** | **§ 713.06(3)(d)** Final Contractor’s Affidavit | **At or before final payment** | Statutory form; sworn; lists unpaid lienors | Owner who pays **without** affidavit loses “proper‑payment” defense and may pay twice |
| **§ 41.007 Homestead warning** | **§ 713.13(1)(e)** NOC bold text re: retainage / releases | Same as NOC trigger | 14‑pt bold heading mandated | Owner who ignores warning risks double payment |
| **RCLA § 27.004 Notice of Defect** | **Chapter 558 pre‑suit notice** | *At least 60 days* pre‑suit (condo 120 days) | Statutory notice of construction defect; certified mail | Filing suit without notice → abatement / fees; failure to respond → statutory defenses lost |

## 3 Compliance Checklist (One‑Pager)
1. **Record & Post NOC** (§ 713.13) *before work starts*.
2. **Serve Notice‑to‑Owner** – subs/suppliers within 45 days (§ 713.06).
3. **Collect Conditional Lien Waivers** with every progress draw (§ 713.20).
4. **Provide Progress Affidavits** on demand (§ 713.06(3)(d)).
5. **File Final Contractor’s Affidavit** before final payment.
6. **Confirm All Releases Received**; then release retainage / final payment.
7. **Record Notice of Termination** once all lienors paid (§ 713.132).
8. **Follow Chapter 558** for any defect claims.

## 5 Common Integration Pitfalls
- Owner forgets to **sign** NOC → lender mortgage may lose priority; subs still protected.
- NOC **expires after 1 year** mid‑project → post‑expiry payments are improper.
- GC fails to **amend NOC** after lender change → subs notice wrong lender, delaying funds.
- Skipping **Final Affidavit** → owner loses proper‑payment defense, double‑pay risk.
- Ignoring **Ch. 558** notice → court abates suit + awards fees.
